# Holy Shoot: translation decisions

Facts and sources: `translations/bible.yaml`. The game has only English, so gender and meaning
come from the text itself.

## Characters

- **The player is Samuel or Toshiko**, so everything the fairy, angels, bosses and descriptions say
  to the player is genderless ("Udało się!", "Ale ci to nie wychodzi", "Już się rumienisz!").
  Samuel's own lines masculine, Toshiko's feminine.
- **Toshiko speaks IT jargon** ("Zdebugowane i usunięte!", "404: nie znaleziono demona!",
  "Uruchamiam niszczyciel_demonów.exe!").
- **The fairy companion is feminine** ("it" in the original): "Ta-dam! Przybyła twoja bohaterka".
  Mouthy as in the original.
- **Bosses with epithets:** Baron Przypalacz (Burnsalot, a hellish baby with a baby tooth and
  pacifier), Lady Miłogeddon (Lovemageddon), Komornik (The Debtkeeper, Mammon; finance jargon:
  "Jesteś pod kreską!", "Towar nie podlega zwrotowi").
- **Ghosts with puns:** Needapu ("need a poo") → Kupulkan (Kukulkan + kupa), so "don't even think
  of joking about my name" still works; Milcah Kow ("milk a cow") → Milka Krasula. Spearatrix stays.

## Opportunities taken

"Demonetized" → "Zdemonetyzowany" (anti-demon perk), "Zapocalypse" → "Porażalipsa", "Crit Happens"
→ "Krytyk się zdarza", "Well, Well, Well" → "No proszę, proszę" (Healing Basin perk), "pyramid
scheme" → "piramidy finansowe" (Mayan ghost).

## Deliberate departures

- Numbers after a colon or before "s" ("Zabij wrogów: {Arg0}."), no numeral declension.
- Original typos/errors (Toshiko "his path", "Defat Baron") are correct in Polish.
- Developer diagnostics (`Steam Stat Saved`, `MapOrigin…`) copied verbatim.
