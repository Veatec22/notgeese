grief under it. Keep fragments as fragments; no smoothing into full sentences.

- **Profanity at the recording's strength:** "Fuck. FUCK." → „Kurwa. KURWA.”; "Good fucking
  riddance." → „No i kurwa dobrze.” "Cholera" would soften an audible line.
- **Draculae, Djedefre, James Savage** keep their form; Draculae indeclinable (feminine on -e:
  „z Draculae”), Djedefre indeclinable. Weapon names (DM11, Strikebreaker, Gullstock, Cavalry,
  Uzi) stay; "Launcher" → „Granatnik”, "Molotovs" → „Mołotowy”.
- **Stakes** = „kołki” („wbić kołek w serce”); **the void** = „pustka” ("The void screams." →
  „Pustka krzyczy.”); ABYSS = „OTCHŁAŃ”, so the two stay apart.
- **Hostages in UI:** "SAVE INNOCENT / INNOCENT RESCUED / INNOCENT KILLED / VICTIM SAVED" →
  „OFIARA” forms („RATUJ OFIARĘ”, „OFIARA URATOWANA”, „OFIARA ZGINĘŁA”): hostages are men and
  women, "ofiara" fits both.
- **Chapter titles** (all caps, idioms): transcreate the function, not the words
  (GRAVEYARD SHIFT → „NOCNA ZMIANA”, BREAK SHIT → „ROZPIERDOL”, PIECE OF MIND pun → Polish pun or
  plain meaning, recorded per title). Latin/proper ones (PURGATORIO, OUROBOROS, VESPER) stay.
- **Split subtitles:** translate the whole sentence, then cut it at the same point of sense; each
  piece must read on its own and match what is being said when it shows.
- **Menus:** Polish Steam/Microsoft terms (Rozdzielczość, Synchronizacja pionowa, Czułość), first
  word capitalized unless the original is all caps; imperative 2nd person for actions
  („Wznów”, „Wyjdź do menu”). "Enter Nightmare" (endless mode) → „Wejdź w Koszmar”.
- **Pill Cop** (radio drama title) → „Glina na Prochach” (settled in the full translation).
