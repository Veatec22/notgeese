# Dread Templar: translation decisions

Translated before the direction/review process existed; decisions were not recorded at the time.
`translations/bible.yaml` (2026-10-02) describes the existing translation after the fact.

## Characters

- **Templar (player): masculine.** First-person cutscene narration and HUD one-liners; swears at
  the original strength („Kurwa! Jestem templariuszem, nie olimpijskim sprinterem.”).
- **Beelsebur: masculine**, addresses the hero „templariuszu”.
- **Captain: masculine**, „nieznajomy”. **Girl looking for Eva: feminine**, „dobry panie”.
