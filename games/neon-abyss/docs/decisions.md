# Neon Abyss: translation decisions

Facts and sources: `translations/bible.yaml`.

## Characters and address

- **Player is genderless** (both genders playable: Anna, Saya, Amir, James…): nothing said to the
  player has gendered past tense or adjectives ("Sześć trafień w tym pomieszczeniu?", "Gdy śmierć
  jest blisko").
- **Secretary: female, "ty" to the player** (RU "Должна сказать", Hades "Talk to her"); player is
  "Pracownik nr 233"; corporate stickler tone.
- **Programmer: male, "wy" to players** (RU "чуть не забыл", "вы"; EN "Hello strangers"). *Sir
  Eat-alot* (Lancelot pun) → **Sir Żarłocelot**.
- **Lans: male investor** (Hades: "what does he expect").
- **Stylist and Bartender: female** (RU "я была", EN "she").
- **Pets without pronouns:** EN gives he/she, Polish names have their own gender (KAKTUSIK with
  "she") → "Rani każdego, kogo dotknie".

## Opportunities taken

- Film quotes in their Polish versions: Blade Runner "Wszystkie te chwile znikną w czasie...",
  Star Wars "To koniec! Stoję wyżej!", The Shining "Sama praca bez zabawy robi z Jacka nudziarza",
  The Little Prince, "TO SPARTA!!!", "Cierpliwości, młody padawanie".
- Kept as Polish players know them: *Git gud*, *Pay to win*, *Another one bites the dust*, *I Believe
  I Can Fly*, *Mamba forever*, Big Daddy, Mjolnir, Silmaril, Fin Funnel.
- "Piątek, trzynastego!" for the midnight mask (EN just "It's Friday!").

## Deliberate departures

- **Elite enemy titles as nouns:** the game glues title + name of either gender ("HUGE LUCY", "HUGE
  BOB") → nicknames OLBRZYM, MIKRUS, PANIKARZ, KAMELEON, ZŁOŚNIK, KLON instead of adjectives.
- Console codes stay English (BACK, OPENDOOR): the game matches them.
- Source typos fixed silently (SOUL BRINGNER, RED DROGAN, MADMASK).
- Names of characters, pets, bosses and elites stay (ANNA, ADAM, PEWDIE, MCTUCKY).
- Short option labels: V-SYNC, "POMOC W CELOWANIU".
