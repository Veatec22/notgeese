# Turbo Overkill: translation decisions

Facts and sources: `translations/bible.yaml`.

## Characters

- **SYN: feminine, name indeclinable** ("bez SYN", "SYN pochłonęła"). Voice Patricia Summersett, RU
  "я пришла", "protect your mother", "her" from S.A.M.M. Where a noun governs ("wirus SYN", "program
  SYN, który…") it agrees with the noun.
- **The Exec (Dyrektor): woman.** Voice DB Cooper, Ripper's "boss lady". Label "Dyrektor" fits both
  genders; lines feminine or impersonal. Cast source: english-voice-over.fandom.com, DB Cooper bio on
  thegdex.com.
- **Ripper: woman, name indeclinable** ("do Ripper"). RU gives her masculine "я убил", but the game
  says "bless her heart": RU treated as an error.
- **S.A.M.M. addresses Johnny "sir" / "pan"** (masculine); everyone else "ty".
- **Vermilion Front captain and Jazz: men** (sergeant's "sir"; "he's been mulched").

| Superior Maw | Udoskonalony Maw | matches the tutorial (boss bar had "Ulepszony") |
| Wallrun Boots | Buty ścianołaza | unified, shorter |

Weapon names (Waster, Boomer, Twincendiary, Telefragger, Instagibber), places (Paradise, Vector-4,
Los Haven) and song titles stay English (titles in „”). Enemy names as in the bestiary. Profanity at
similar strength.

## Opportunities taken

- SYN/sin pun: "Kochacie SYN, co? Dobrze... to macie tu syna skurwysyna!"
- Source error: `coop_prompt_joinroomfailed` says "Create Room Failed"; PL follows the key ("Nie
  udało się dołączyć do pokoju").
- Finale "system32? Tu odpalę polecenie usuwania": joke kept, readable for Polish players.

## Deliberate departures

- "Hit Markers" twice: "Dźwięki trafień" (audio setting) and "Znaczniki trafień" (HUD cross); the
  setting descriptions show two options.
- Ammo counter "Naboje", infodex "Naboje do strzelby".
- Hand-broken lines in four recorded conversations joined; the field wraps.
