# I Am Your Beast: translation decisions

Facts and sources: `translations/bible.yaml`. Speakers in scenes were set from exchange order and
segment color scheme (7231/7236 Harding, 7233 Burkin, 7234 Byron); the game has no name labels.

## Direction (agreed with user 2026-09-25)

1. **Harding's voice: dry, ironic politeness.** Calm, short or deliberately over-polite; rarely
   swears, so "Ffffuck." → "Kuuurwa." has weight. Not an action-movie hardman.
   "I'm whittling a bird out of soap, Charles." → "Strugam ptaka z mydła, Charles." *Rejected:*
   "Rzeźbię w mydle ptaszka" (the diminutive adds infantile comedy; he means the bird seriously).
2. **Forms of address.** Harding ↔ Burkin "ty" both ways (old acquaintances; distance through
   titles: "generale", mocking "Charles"; Burkin: "Harding", "synu"). Byron → Harding "pan" in the
   first call only (6 Help Wanted: "Mister, uh... Mister Alphonse Harding?"), then "ty" and
   "Alphonse". *Rejected:* "pan generał" with formal forms; Byron "ty" from the start; Byron "pan"
   throughout. Names decline (Hardinga, Burkinowi); vocative = nominative ("Harding!").
3. **Level and scene names adapted:** Walkout → Odejście, Tête-à-tête, Starting Gun → Strzał
   startowy, Mind the Gap → Uwaga na przepaść, On Your Six → Na szóstej, Javert i Valjean,
   **Wabbit Season → Sezon na kłólika** (Elmer Fudd; a soldier calls "kici, kici"), **I Am Your
   Beast → Jestem twoją bestią** (last level; logo stays English), I Am Your Fears → Jestem twoim
   strachem. *Rejected:* "Sezon polowań"; the English title as a level name.
4. **Profanity of the same strength** (agent, as in Wild Bastards, Katana ZERO, Laika), no
   homophobic slurs: "FUCK your access code!" → "PIERDOLĘ twój kod dostępu!", "Employee of the
   motherfucking month!" → "Pracownik, kurwa, miesiąca!", "Come on out, ya big bitch!" → "No
   wyłaź, ty cipo!".
5. **Segmented lines and growing sentences** (agent, technical and editorial): each segment
   translated so Polish word order cuts at the same points; growing sentences keep the start
   verbatim ("Widzisz, / Widzisz, ja tak nie uważam."). Stutters, "--" and stage directions
   (`*sigh*` → `*westchnienie*`) stay.
6. **Terms.** **COI → I.T.O.** (user; Covert Operations Initiative → Inicjatywa Tajnych Operacji;
   indeclinable, feminine: "I.T.O. zrobiła ze mnie…"; dots after the vertical because "ITO" read
   as a word). one last job → ostatnia robota; NATO alphabet stays; Dear diary → Drogi pamiętniku;
   Stomp → zdeptanie, Kick → kopnięcie, Quick Turn → szybki obrót, Perch → "Przyczaj się", Kill
   Confirms → potwierdzenia zabójstw, poultice bushes → ziołowe krzaki; grades S–D stay; credits:
   roles translated, names and studios unchanged.
